PaisaJeeto Delete Your Account

Last updated: 4 November 2026

You can delete your PaisaJeeto account at any time, in either of the two ways below. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1 — Delete instantly in the app

Open PaisaJeeto, go to the Wallet tab, and tap Delete account under your signed-in email. Confirm, and your account is deleted immediately — no waiting, no email needed. For security, the app may ask you to enter your password once more before finishing.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the same email address you registered with:

Contact: paisajeeto3@gmail.com — subject: "Delete my account"

We verify that the request comes from the account's registered email address and process it within 7 days, deleting the account and profile from Firebase (our sign-in and account-data provider). We may reply once to confirm the deletion is done.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in account (email address and password record) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your account profile: email address, referral code, coin-balance mirror and notification (FCM) token.
  • Your coin balance, referral code and in-app history, which are removed with the account. Any pending payout request is cancelled.

What may be kept

Records of payouts that have already been paid may be retained for accounting and legal purposes. Anonymous counters that contain no personal information (for example, the total number of referrals a code produced) may also remain.
