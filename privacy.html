PaisaJeeto Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

PaisaJeeto ("the app") is a free play-and-earn mini-games app. This policy explains, in plain language, what information the app does and does not handle. By using PaisaJeeto you agree to this policy.

1. No accounts, no sign-up

PaisaJeeto has no user accounts. You never create a username or password, and the app never asks for your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal details just to play.

2. Your game data stays on your phone

Your coin balance, game progress, daily play counts, spin history and settings are stored only on your own device (in the app's local storage). They are not sent to us or to any server. If you uninstall the app or clear its data, this information is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.

3. Information we collect

The app itself collects no personal data. We run no analytics, no tracking, and no user profiling inside the app. The app connects to the internet only to download its game configuration (game list, rewards and settings) from our configuration host.

4. Advertising

The current version of PaisaJeeto shows placeholder (dummy) ad screens and does not include a third-party advertising network. When advertising is enabled, ads will be provided by Google AdMob. Google may then collect and use an advertising identifier and related data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy. We do not control the data Google collects through AdMob.

5. Payout requests

Coins in PaisaJeeto are free, have no purchase price, cannot be bought, and are never sold. If you choose to redeem coins, you submit a payout request through a form and provide your UPI ID or Paytm number yourself. Those details are used only to process that payout manually (normally within 48 hours). They are not used for marketing and are not sold or shared with third parties, except the payment service needed to send your payout.

6. Children

PaisaJeeto is intended for users aged 18 and above. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly accept payout details from anyone under 18.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

8. Spin Wheel Rules

The spin wheel in PaisaJeeto is completely free — no purchase is necessary, ever, and spins can never be bought. You receive free spins each day (and can earn extra spins by watching an ad). Every spin lands on one segment of the wheel with the following fixed chances:

  • 5 coins — 22%
  • 10 coins — 20%
  • 0 coins — 14%
  • 20 coins — 15%
  • 15 coins — 13%
  • 50 coins — 8%
  • 5 coins — 5%
  • 100 coins — 3%

These same odds are also shown in the app, directly under the spin wheel. Coins redeem at a fixed rate of 100 coins = ₹1, as stated in the app's wallet.
